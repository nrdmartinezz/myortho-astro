**What insurance do you accept?**

Insurance laws are changing all the time, and plans change even quicker. For that reason, you should speak with someone in our office directly when you are ready to begin treatment. No worries. We help a lot of adults who have not seen a dentist in a while (as well as kids coming to the dentist for the first time). We will explain to you what’s going on with your mouth, what we recommend, and what we plan to do about before we put any plan into action.  In addition, here are some [brushing tips before visiting](https://health.usnews.com/health-news/health-wellness/articles/2014/10/01/6-brushing-tips-to-impress-your-dentist).
