**Do you need a periodontist?**

The mouth might seem like a minor part of the body, but it is actually filled with complexity and **hugely important for your overall health**. That’s why there are so many unique kinds of doctors that work on the area in and around the mouth – dentists, orthodontist, oral surgeons etc.

One type of specialist that often gets overlooked is the periodontist. If you have never heard of one, don’t worry. But if you have never heard of one, also read on, because getting the best treatment for your oral health depends on seeing the **right kind of specialist**. Learn the answer to the question ‘what is a periodontist’ so you can make informed decisions about your oral health.

### What is a Periodontist?

A periodontist is a doctor that specifically treats issues affecting the **gums and the bones of the mouth**. These kinds of issues are often referred to together as periodontitis, or periodontal disease. These specialists attend regular dental school and then receive an additional three years of schooling to develop their expertise in these specific features of the mouth.

### What Does a Periodontist Treat?

Your general dentist has probably talked to your about your gums before. Patients are typically only referred to a [periodontist](/services/) when extreme issues like advanced gingivitis begin to comprise their gums and bones. In addition to **preventing, diagnosing, and treating [periodontal disease](http://www.nidcr.nih.gov/oralhealth/Topics/GumDiseases/PeriodontalGumDisease.htm)**, periodontists install dental implants and are involved with select cosmetic procedures.

### Should I See a Periodontist?

In general you should only see a periodontist when **referred to one by your general dentist**. The doctor you see for regular cleanings and checkups may be able to resolve your issues faster and at a lower cost. But if you are referred to a periodontal specialist, it is important to heed the recommendation.

In these cases, a specialist will be able to give you the best advice about your long-term health.

### What Should I Expect at the Periodontist?

Your first visit will probably be a lot like a visit to any dentist. The doctor will closely examine your teeth and gums and carefully **review your medical history** to identify any drug interactions or previous conditions that could be affecting your problem. The doctor will then offer a diagnosis and recommend a further course of treatment. This treatment could be as simple as changing your oral health routine, could involve prescription medication, and in extreme cases may require surgery.
