## What Is Phase 1 Orthodontic Treatment?

Phase 1 orthodontic treatment is an early intervention approach designed to address dental and skeletal issues while your child’s mouth is still developing. Typically initiated between ages 7 and 10, this treatment phase focuses on guiding jaw growth, correcting bite problems, and creating space for permanent teeth to erupt properly.

Unlike traditional orthodontic treatment that often begins after all permanent teeth have come in, Phase 1 treatment addresses developing concerns early, potentially reducing the need for more extensive procedures later.

## When Should You Consider Early Orthodontic Treatment?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. At MyOrthodontist, we believe timely assessments allow us to identify issues before they become more complicated.

Common signs that your child may benefit from Phase 1 treatment include:

-   Crowded or misplaced front teeth
-   Difficulty chewing or biting
-   Protruding teeth, overbites, or underbites
-   Jaw shifting or misalignment when biting
-   Speech difficulties related to dental structure
-   Persistent thumb sucking or mouth breathing habits

Early intervention allows us to guide growth when your child’s mouth and jaw are most responsive to treatment.

## Benefits of Phase 1 Treatment for Ages 7–10

Early orthodontic treatment offers several advantages that support both oral health and confidence.

| Benefit | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Guides Jaw Growth | Helps correct skeletal imbalances and promotes proper jaw development before growth is complete. |
| Creates Space for Permanent Teeth | Reduces severe crowding and may lessen the need for future extractions. |
| Improves Bite and Function | Addresses crossbites, open bites, and other issues that affect chewing and speech. |
| Enhances Self-Esteem | Early improvements can boost confidence during important developmental years. |
| Reduces Future Treatment Time | May simplify and shorten comprehensive orthodontic treatment later on. |

By addressing these issues early, we help lay the foundation for a lifetime of healthy smiles.

## What to Expect During Phase 1 Treatment at MyOrthodontist

Every child’s smile is unique, which is why we provide exceptional individualized care tailored to their needs. Depending on your child’s condition, treatment may include:

-   Palatal expanders or space maintainers to guide jaw development
-   Partial braces or clear aligners on selected teeth
-   Habit appliances to discourage thumb sucking or tongue thrusting
-   Routine progress visits every 6–8 weeks

Most Phase 1 treatment plans last between six months and one year. Throughout treatment, our Fayetteville team works to make every appointment comfortable, positive, and fun.
