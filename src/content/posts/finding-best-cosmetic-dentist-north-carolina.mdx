Cosmetic dentistry has come a long way in recent years. Today’s procedures are faster, quicker, and more affordable than ever before, and they produce better results. That’s why so many people are seeking out subtle corrective treatments to enhance and restore the beauty of their smile.

The only tricky part is locating the best cosmetic dentist in North Carolina. Lots of dentists now offer cosmetic procedures, and evaluating their services, prices, and credentials can be overwhelming. We want you to feel confident about your cosmetic procedure and excited about the upgrade your smile is about to undergo. That’s why we’ve made a list of features that identify the best cosmetic dentists in North Carolina:

-   **Selection** – You don’t want to have to go to more than one cosmetic dentist. That’s why you should seek out a provider that offers a wide variety of services to address every conceivable cosmetic issue.
-   **Affordability –** Renewing your smile shouldn’t have to cost you an arm and a leg. Look for a cosmetic dentist that offers competitive prices and makes financing options available.
-   **Healthcare –** Ideally, your cosmetic dentist would also be your general dentist, or be connected to your general dentist. That way you can address cosmetic issues and oral health at the same time, and take steps to enhance both together.
-   **Photos –** Any good cosmetic dentist in North Carolina will be excited to show you before and after pictures of their work. That way you can get a better sense of what each procedure entails and what kind results you can expect.
-   **Consultations –** Before you commit to any cosmetic procedure, your dentists should have a consultation with you to help you understand and explore your options in depth.
-   **Emergency Care –** Cosmetic procedures are largely risk-free, but in the very unlikely event that issues do arise after leaving the dentist’s office, you want to know that emergency dental care is available as soon as you need it.
