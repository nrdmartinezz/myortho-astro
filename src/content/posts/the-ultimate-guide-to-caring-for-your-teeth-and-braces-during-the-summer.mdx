## 1\. Why Summer Is a Critical Time for Orthodontic Care

## Routine Disruption

Summer means changes in routine. With school out and schedules more flexible, it’s easy to let good habits slip. Sleepovers, vacations, and spontaneous outings can all disrupt your regular oral hygiene and care schedule. Consistency is key for successful orthodontic treatment—missing just a few days of proper care can set your progress back.

## Dietary Temptations

Summer is full of tempting treats—ice cream, popcorn at the movies, sticky candies at the fair. Many of these can damage braces or make aligner care difficult. Knowing what to avoid and what to enjoy is half the battle.

## Travel and Outdoor Activities

More time away from home means new challenges for oral hygiene. Whether you’re hiking in the mountains, traveling to the coast, or camping in a state park, it’s important to plan ahead for your orthodontic needs.

## The MyOrthodontist Perspective

Our team sees more emergency visits in the summer due to broken brackets, lost aligners, and forgotten appointments. That’s why we’ve created this guide—to help you avoid the most common pitfalls and keep your treatment on track.

## 2\. Summer Foods: What to Enjoy and What to Avoid

## Braces-Friendly Foods

Summer brings a bounty of delicious, braces-safe foods. Here are some favorites:

-   Soft Fruits: Watermelon, berries, bananas, peaches, and nectarines are all gentle on braces.
-   Yogurt and Smoothies: Cool, creamy, and easy to customize with your favorite fruits.
-   Grilled Vegetables: Zucchini, bell peppers, and mushrooms are soft and flavorful.
-   Pasta Salads: Skip the crunchy toppings and enjoy soft pasta with veggies and dressing.
-   Ice Cream and Frozen Yogurt: Enjoy in moderation, and avoid hard mix-ins like nuts or candy.

## Foods to Avoid

Some summer staples can wreak havoc on your braces or aligners:

-   Sticky Candies: Caramel, taffy, and gummy candies can pull off brackets and wires.
-   Popcorn: Kernels can get stuck and damage appliances.
-   Corn on the Cob: Cut the kernels off before eating.
-   Hard Ice Cubes and Crunchy Snacks: Don’t chew ice or hard chips.
-   Sugary Drinks: Soda and sweet teas can increase the risk of cavities, especially if oral hygiene slips.

## Tips for Healthy Choices

-   Plan Ahead: Bring braces-friendly snacks to parties and outings.
-   Stay Hydrated: Water is the best choice for your teeth and overall health.
-   Rinse After Eating: If you can’t brush right away, rinse your mouth with water.

## Braces-Safe Summer Recipes

-   Berry Smoothie: Blend berries, banana, yogurt, and a splash of milk.
-   Grilled Veggie Skewers: Thread soft veggies onto skewers and grill until tender.
-   Frozen Yogurt Bark: Spread yogurt on a tray, top with soft fruit, freeze, and break into pieces.

## 3\. Traveling with Braces or Aligners: Tips for Success

## Packing Essentials

Before you hit the road, make sure you have everything you need:

-   Orthodontic wax
-   Travel toothbrush and toothpaste
-   Floss threaders or orthodontic flossers
-   Small bottle of mouthwash
-   Aligner case (if using Invisalign or similar)
-   Extra elastics (if prescribed)
-   Interdental brushes

## On-the-Go Hygiene

-   Brush and Floss After Every Meal: Even when you’re away from home.
-   Use Bottled Water: If you’re camping or traveling, use bottled water for rinsing and brushing.
-   Keep Aligners Safe: Always store them in their case when not in use.

## Emergencies on the Road

-   Lost Aligner: Move to the next in your series if you’re close to the scheduled change; otherwise, wear the previous one and call us for advice.
-   Broken Bracket or Wire: Use wax to cover sharp edges and call MyOrthodontist as soon as possible.

## International Travel

-   Contact Information: Keep our phone numbers and email addresses handy in case you need to reach us from abroad.
-   Virtual Consultations: Ask about telehealth options if you’ll be away for an extended period.

## 4\. Summer Sports and Braces: Staying Safe and Active

## The Importance of Mouthguards

Sports are a big part of summer, but they can be risky for orthodontic patients. A custom-fitted mouthguard is essential for:

-   Soccer
-   Basketball
-   Baseball
-   Skateboarding
-   Mountain biking

## Choosing the Right Mouthguard

-   Custom Mouthguards: Best fit and protection, available through our office.
-   Boil-and-Bite Mouthguards: Available at sporting goods stores, but may not fit as well with braces.

## Tips for Active Kids and Teens

-   Don’t Skip the Mouthguard: Even non-contact sports can result in falls or collisions.
-   Check for Damage: Inspect your braces and mouthguard after each game.

## Swimming and Water Activities

-   Chlorine and Oral Health: Rinse your mouth after swimming in chlorinated pools.
-   Lost Aligners: Never leave aligners by the pool; always use a case.

## 5\. Oral Hygiene on the Go: Keeping Your Smile Bright

## Daily Routine

-   Brush at Least Twice a Day: Three times is even better during orthodontic treatment.
-   Floss Daily: Use floss threaders or orthodontic flossers.
-   Use Mouthwash: An antimicrobial rinse can help reduce plaque.

## Dealing with Sticky Situations

-   After S’mores or Ice Cream: Rinse your mouth and brush as soon as possible.
-   Portable Kits: Keep a small kit in your bag or car.

## Recommended Products

-   Electric Toothbrush: More effective at removing plaque.
-   Interdental Brushes: Great for cleaning around brackets and wires.
-   Fluoride Toothpaste: Strengthens enamel and helps prevent cavities.

## 6\. Managing Orthodontic Emergencies During Summer

## Common Issues

-   Loose Wires or Brackets: Use wax to cover sharp edges and call us.
-   Lost or Broken Retainers/Aligners: Contact us ASAP for replacements.
-   Soreness or Ulcers: Saltwater rinses and wax can provide relief.

## First Aid Tips

-   Orthodontic Wax: Cover any irritating areas.
-   Saltwater Rinse: Mix 1 teaspoon of salt in 8 ounces of warm water.
-   Pain Relief: Over-the-counter pain relievers can help with soreness.

## When to Call MyOrthodontist

If you experience significant pain, bleeding, or have a broken appliance, call us right away. We offer emergency appointments at all 14 of our North Carolina locations.

## 9\. The Importance of Hydration and Oral Health

## Why Hydration Matters

-   Saliva Production: Staying hydrated helps maintain saliva, which protects your teeth.
-   Rinsing Away Food Debris: Water helps flush away sugars and acids.

## Best Drinks for Summer

-   Water: Always the best choice.
-   Milk: Good for teeth, but brush after flavored varieties.
-   Avoid: Soda, sports drinks, and sweet teas.

## Tips for Staying Hydrated

-   Carry a reusable water bottle.
-   Add slices of fruit for natural flavor.
-   Set reminders to drink water throughout the day.

## 11\. FAQs: Summer Orthodontic Care

Can I swim with braces or aligners?
Yes! Just be sure to wear a mouthguard for sports and keep aligners in their case when not in use.

What if I miss an appointment?
Call or email us as soon as possible to reschedule. Missing appointments can delay your treatment.

How do I handle discomfort while traveling?
Use orthodontic wax, saltwater rinses, and over-the-counter pain relievers. Contact us if the issue persists.

Are there any summer foods I should absolutely avoid?
Sticky candies, popcorn, and hard snacks should be avoided. When in doubt, ask us!

Can I have ice cream with braces?
Yes, but avoid hard mix-ins and brush after enjoying your treat.
