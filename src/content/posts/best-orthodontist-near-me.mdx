#### The Latest Treatments and Technologies

When you go in for that first free orthodontist consultation, ask about the various treatment options available. See if the orthodontist is following the newest trends and selecting technology that makes treatment quicker and more painless. For example, do they offer [Invisalign](/blog/invisalign-faq/) as well as [traditional braces](/types-of-braces/)? Do they keep up with trends in accelerated orthodontics?

#### Elite Preferred Provider Status

If you are looking for an Invisalign provider specifically, ask if they have an Elite Preferred Provider status. To be an Elite Preferred Provider, an orthodontist must have the highest level of Invisalign case experience – only about 2% of Invisalign providers across North America have the level of experience to qualify. Having this distinction helps patients find an Invisalign provider they can trust – one that has the knowledge and experience to create beautiful smiles.
