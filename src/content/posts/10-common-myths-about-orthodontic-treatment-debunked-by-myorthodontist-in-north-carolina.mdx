In reality, modern orthodontics addresses much more than just straightening teeth—it can improve bite function, jaw alignment, and even overall oral health. **Myth #1:  Orthodontic Treatment is Only for Kids and Teens**

Many people assume that once you reach adulthood, it’s too late to start orthodontic treatment or improve your smile. This myth is rooted in the idea that orthodontic treatment is most effective during the teenage years when the jaw is still growing. While it’s true that early treatment can be advantageous in some cases, age alone is not a barrier to achieving excellent results.

The reality is that orthodontic treatment can be successful at any age. In fact, a growing number of adults are choosing Invisalign and other orthodontic solutions to address dental issues that were never corrected in childhood—or that developed later in life. As long as your teeth and gums are healthy, you’re a candidate for orthodontic care.

At MyOrthodontist, we proudly treat patients of all ages. Dr. Moray offers personalized treatment plans that fit seamlessly into adult lifestyles, often using Invisalign for a discreet, comfortable approach. Whether you’re in your 30s, 50s, or beyond, we’re here to help you smile with confidence.

**Myth #2: Orthodontic Treatment Is Purely Cosmetic**

Some people think orthodontics is just about vanity—a way to get picture-perfect teeth. While a beautiful smile is certainly a benefit, orthodontics is about so much more than appearance. Misaligned teeth and bite issues can lead to serious oral health problems if left untreated.

Crooked teeth can be harder to clean, increasing the risk of cavities, gum disease, and even tooth loss. A misaligned bite can lead to uneven tooth wear, jaw pain, and speech difficulties. Orthodontic treatment helps correct these problems, creating not only a straighter smile but a healthier mouth.

At MyOrthodontist, we take a comprehensive approach to care. Dr. Moray evaluates each patient’s bite, jaw function, and oral health before recommending a treatment plan. Whatever custom treatment plan our team creates for you, you can trust that your treatment will support both aesthetics and long-term wellness.

**Myth #3: Treatment is Always Painful**

Many people fear that orthodontic treatment will be painful and inconvenient. Stories about sore mouths and constant discomfort have discouraged countless patients from seeking care. While some tenderness is common during the adjustment period, orthodontic pain is typically minimal and temporary.

Thanks to advances in technology, modern orthodontic appliances are designed for greater comfort. Invisalign aligners use gentle, controlled pressure to move teeth without wires or brackets. Even traditional braces have improved with smaller brackets and more efficient archwires.

At MyOrthodontist, patient comfort is a top priority. Dr. Moray uses cutting-edge tools and gentle techniques to minimize discomfort at every stage. Our patients often tell us how pleasantly surprised they are by how easy treatment feels compared to what they expected.

**Myth #4: Invisalign Doesn’t Work as Well as Braces**

Some people assume that Invisalign is a cosmetic option that can’t handle “real” orthodontic problems. While it’s true that clear aligners were once limited to minor cases, today’s Invisalign technology can treat a wide range of dental issues—including crowding, spacing, overbites, underbites, and crossbites.

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made aligners to gradually move your teeth into their ideal position. With SmartTrack material and advanced planning software, Invisalign can be just as effective as braces for many patients, especially when monitored by a skilled orthodontist.

Dr. Moray is a certified Invisalign provider with years of experience designing precise, customized treatment plans. He uses digital scanning and virtual monitoring to track your progress and make adjustments when needed—helping patients in North Carolina achieve outstanding results without metal braces.

**Myth #5: Once Invisalign Comes Off, Teeth Stay Straight Forever**

It’s easy to think that once your Invisalign Clear Aligners are removed or you’ve completed your treatment, your orthodontic journey is over for good. However, teeth are constantly shifting throughout life, and without proper maintenance, they can slowly drift back out of alignment.

That’s why retainers are essential after any orthodontic treatment. They help hold your teeth in their new position while the surrounding bone and tissue stabilize. Skipping your retainer—even for a short time—can undo months or years of progress.

At MyOrthodontist, we educate every patient about post-treatment care. Dr. Moray provides high-quality retainers and clear instructions on how to wear them for long-term success. Our team also offers follow-up appointments to ensure your smile stays on track for years to come.

**Myth #6: Dentists and Orthodontists Offer the Same Services**

While general dentists are skilled in many areas of oral health, orthodontists undergo additional training specifically focused on diagnosing and correcting alignment and bite issues. This distinction is crucial when choosing the right provider for your smile.

Orthodontists complete two to three extra years of specialized education beyond dental school. They focus exclusively on moving teeth and aligning jaws. While some dentists offer orthodontic services, they may not have the same depth of knowledge or experience.

Dr. Moray is a board-certified orthodontist with a passion for excellence. At our North Carolina office, patients receive the benefit of advanced training and years of focused orthodontic care. When it comes to achieving precise, long-lasting results, specialized expertise makes all the difference.

**Myth #7: You Can’t Play Sports or Instruments During Treatment**

Many young patients worry that starting treatment will force them to give up their favorite hobbies, especially sports or playing musical instruments. While it’s true that some adjustments may be needed, orthodontic treatment at MyOrthodontist won’t stop you from doing what you love.

With a traditional orthodontic treatment option like braces, athletes may require a custom mouthguard to protect their teeth and braces during contact sports, but Invisalign offers the option to remove your trays or wear a custom mouthguard during practices and games. Musicians, especially those who play wind instruments, won’t have any trouble adjusting during treatment because they also have the option to remove hair aligners during practice and performances.

At MyOrthodontist, we support our patients’ lifestyles. We offer guidance on how to protect your teeth during physical activities and tips for musicians adjusting to treatment. Our goal is to help every patient maintain a full, active life throughout their orthodontic journey.

**Myth #8: Orthodontic Treatment Severely Limits What You Can Eat**

Treatment typically comes with dietary guidelines, but our office provides Invisalign Clear Aligners, which are a removable treatment option! The removable nature of these trays allows patients to take out their trays before eating, so you can enjoy your favorite foods while you achieve the smile of your dreams! Many patients think they’ll have to give up all their favorite foods, but with a little creativity and care, you can still enjoy a satisfying and varied diet.

Sticky, crunchy, and hard foods need to be avoided with braces to prevent damage to brackets and wires. However, patients have the freedom to indulge in all of their favorite meals with Invisalign. We suggest eating a variety of fruits, vegetables, protein, carbs, and healthy fats to obtain a balanced diet, but your trays won’t limit your food options or ability to eat.

At MyOrthodontist, we prioritize convenience and try to make your treatment experience as enjoyable as possible. Choosing Invisalign at MyOrthodontist means choosing excellent treatment that fits into your lifestyle. Remove your aligners before each meal or snack, and just remember to floss and brush before reinserting your aligners again.

**Myth #10: Orthodontics Is Just About Straightening Teeth**

While a straight smile is one of the most visible benefits, orthodontic treatment impacts far more than just how your teeth look. It’s about correcting the underlying structure of your bite and jaw to support better function, comfort, and long-term oral health.

A poorly aligned bite can cause jaw pain, headaches, speech issues, and even difficulty chewing. Orthodontic treatment addresses these functional problems while also creating a more balanced and attractive smile. It’s about total dental wellness—not just appearance.
