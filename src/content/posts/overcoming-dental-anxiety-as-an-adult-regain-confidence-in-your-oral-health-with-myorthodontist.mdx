Dental anxiety affects millions of adults, yet many suffer in silence, delaying or avoiding the care they need. At **MyOrthodontist** in Fayetteville, NC, we understand how overwhelming dental visits can feel—and we’re here to help you break free from fear and take control of your smile.

### Why Dental Anxiety Persists and How It Affects Your Oral Health

Studies show that nearly three-quarters of adults experience some degree of dental fear, with over a quarter reporting severe anxiety. Often, these fears stem from difficult or painful experiences in childhood or early dental visits. Negative memories such as discomfort, feeling unheard, or rushed by providers can create lasting apprehension about returning to the dentist.

This anxiety frequently leads to avoidance. Skipping routine checkups and orthodontic care allows problems to worsen, resulting in more complex—and often more intimidating—treatments down the line. This cycle reinforces the fear and makes future visits even harder. At MyOrthodontist, we recognize this pattern and prioritize creating a welcoming, patient-centered atmosphere to help you feel safe and respected every step of the way.

### How Today’s Orthodontic Care Eases Dental Anxiety

Orthodontic treatment has evolved tremendously, making it more comfortable and less stressful than ever before. Our Fayetteville practice utilizes advanced technology designed to minimize discomfort and enhance patient confidence.

For example, traditional, messy impressions are replaced by our **digital 3D scanning technology**, which quickly and painlessly captures accurate images of your teeth—no gooey trays or gagging. We also offer discreet options like **Invisalign clear aligners**, which are smooth, removable, and custom-made for your smile. These aligners provide a flexible, low-profile alternative to metal braces, helping many adults feel more in control and less anxious throughout treatment.

Beyond technology, our team—including Dr. Larry J. Moray, Dr. Gisela Borget, Dr. Elizabeth Ottey, and others—is dedicated to open, compassionate communication. We take time to explain your treatment plan thoroughly, answer your questions, and ensure you never feel rushed or judged. This supportive approach is proven to reduce dental anxiety and build trust.
