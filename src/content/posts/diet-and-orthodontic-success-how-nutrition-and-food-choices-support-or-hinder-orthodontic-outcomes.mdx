## Why Diet Matters During Orthodontic Treatment

Braces and clear aligners apply gentle, consistent pressure to guide your teeth into their ideal positions. Healthy teeth, gums, and bone tissue are essential for this process to work efficiently. Proper nutrition provides the vitamins and minerals your body needs for bone remodeling, tissue repair, and maintaining strong enamel.

Poor dietary choices, on the other hand, can increase the risk of cavities, gum inflammation, and appliance damage—problems that may interrupt treatment and prolong your time in braces or aligners.

## Foods to Avoid During Orthodontic Treatment

Certain foods can damage orthodontic appliances or make it more difficult to maintain excellent oral hygiene. Avoiding these foods can help keep your treatment on schedule.

| Food Category | Examples | Why Avoid Them? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hard Foods | Nuts, popcorn kernels, hard candies, ice | Can break brackets and bend wires |
| Sticky Foods | Caramel, taffy, chewing gum | Stick to appliances and increase cavity risk |
| Crunchy Foods | Chips, whole apples, raw carrots | May loosen or damage orthodontic hardware |
| Sugary Snacks & Drinks | Soda, candy, sports drinks | Promote plaque buildup and tooth decay |
| Chewy Foods | Bagels, tough meats, licorice | Create excessive pressure on braces |

Patients using clear aligners should also remember to remove their aligners before eating and brush before putting them back in to avoid trapping food and bacteria against the teeth.

## Nutritional Choices That Support a Healthy Smile

A nutrient-rich diet helps your body adapt to orthodontic treatment while keeping your teeth and gums healthy. Choosing softer foods doesn’t mean sacrificing nutrition.

-   **Calcium-rich foods:** Milk, yogurt, cheese, and leafy greens strengthen enamel and support healthy bone structure.
-   **Vitamin C:** Strawberries, oranges, kiwi, and bell peppers help maintain healthy gums and aid tissue repair.
-   **Lean proteins:** Chicken, fish, eggs, beans, and tofu provide amino acids necessary for healing and growth.
-   **Whole grains:** Oatmeal, brown rice, and whole wheat pasta supply energy and important nutrients.
-   **Hydration:** Drinking plenty of water helps rinse away food particles and supports saliva production, which naturally protects teeth.

A balanced diet helps your mouth stay healthy throughout treatment and contributes to a smoother orthodontic experience.

## Tips for Eating Comfortably With Braces or Aligners

It’s normal to experience some tenderness after adjustments or when starting treatment. These simple habits can help make meals more comfortable:

-   Cut foods into smaller pieces to minimize pressure on teeth.
-   Choose softer foods like yogurt, pasta, soups, mashed potatoes, smoothies, and steamed vegetables.
-   Avoid very hot or cold foods if your teeth feel sensitive.
-   Brush and floss after meals to remove food particles around brackets and wires.
-   Use orthodontic wax to cover areas causing irritation inside your cheeks or lips.

Most patients find that these adjustments quickly become part of their normal routine.
