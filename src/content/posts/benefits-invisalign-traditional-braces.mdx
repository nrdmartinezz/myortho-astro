Getting braces is not an exciting prospect for anyone. But **what if we told you that you could permanently correct your alignment issues without experiencing any of the drawbacks of traditional braces?** Discover the benefits of Invisalign – an alternative that is revolutionizing the way people think about straightening teeth – and decide if this is the better option for you.

### 6 Benefits of Invisalign You Should Consider

#### Eat Whatever You Want

With traditional braces, you have to avoid eating foods like popcorn, candy, and chewing gum because they can easily become stuck next to your teeth. With Invisalign, **you are free to eat whatever you want** and never have to worry about your diet damaging your straighteners.

#### Remove the Device at Anytime

Once traditional braces are installed they can only be removed by a professional, and once they are off your treatment is effectively over. **You can take Invisalign straighteners out wherever you need to** and put them back in without compromising your treatment.

#### Overcome the Embarrassment of Braces

When someone is wearing traditional braces, they are hard not to notice on the person’s teeth. That is a frequent source of anxiety and embarrassment. One of the major benefits of Invisalign straighteners is that they are nearly invisible and can be worn without detection.

#### Improve Your Oral Health

Traditional braces make it difficult to brush and floss properly. You end up creating one oral health problem while you are trying to fix another. Since Invisalign straighteners can be removed at any time, you can **continue to take good care of your teeth**.

#### Avoid Pain and Irritation

The metal wires in traditional braces are notorious for causing pain, discomfort, and mouth sores. And since the treatment is long-term, patients are forced to live in discomfort for months. Invi**salign straighteners are made of smooth plastic that is carefully molded to your teeth for a comfortable, pain-free fit.**

#### Visit the Dentist Less Often

Traditional braces require frequent visits to the dentist for tightening and correction. And since they compromise your oral health, you might also need more cleanings and intensive treatments. **Patients who choose Invisalign straighteners instead typically only need to see a dentist every four to six weeks.** That is an easy commitment to fit into a busy schedule.
