As we step into the new year, it’s time to embrace change and strive toward the best version of yourself. January is typically filled with dreams, aspirations, goals, and lists of New Year’s resolutions. With the help of MyOrthodontist, you can add a new smile to your list of 2024 goals. Our team can help you transform your smile and grow your confidence with our cutting-edge orthodontic methods, advanced technology, and personalized treatment plans. 

Dr. Moray can improve the look of your smile as well as your overall oral health. It’s true, that orthodontics can completely change the aesthetic of your smile, but this New Year’s goal goes far beyond looks. We have compiled Six Reasons to Invest in Orthodontic Treatment as a part of your 2024 goals. 

-   **Enhanced  Aesthetics**

Most of our patients come into our office and are excited about the aesthetic impact orthodontic treatment will have on their smile. What a lot of our patients don’t realize though, is that orthodontic treatment has significantly changed and improved over the years. Modern orthodontic advancements offer several discreet and more stylish treatment options, so you can feel comfortable in your new smile and throughout your treatment experience.

-   **Boosted Confidence** 

Your smile is an accessory you wear every single day, and getting braces or clear aligners allows you to confidently show off your smile. Orthodontic issues such as overbites, underbites, cross bites, open bites, spacing, and crowding issues can all take a toll on your confidence. These orthodontic issues can obviously negatively affect the confidence you have in your appearance, but they can also impact your speech, social interactions, and even your professional or academic life.  

-   **Personalized Treatment Plans**

Orthodontic treatment continues to advance over the years and at MyOrthodontist we are dedicated to using the newest and most effective technology and treatment methods. Advancements in braces and Invisalign technology have allowed us to take a more personalized approach, tailoring treatment to each patient’s specific needs and lifestyle. Treatments are more comfortable, more discreet, and more convenient than ever before. Dr. Moray will create a customized treatment plan for you and even allow you to play a role in deciding what option is best for you.  

-   **Improved Oral Health**

The improvements in your overall oral health are the most important changes straight, properly aligned teeth can give you. Our Braces and Invisalign Clear Aligners can simplify your oral hygiene routine and reduce your risk of cavities and gum disease. 

Crooked teeth provide bacteria with the perfect hiding spot, so when we straighten your teeth, it makes them easier to clean. Straight teeth are also less prone to wear and tear, reducing the risk of tooth injury, sensitivity, and erosion. 

-   **Aligned Bite**

Orthodontic treatment can do more than straighten your smile and close unwanted gaps between teeth. It can also drastically improve the alignment and functionality of your bite. A misaligned bite can cause several issues like facial discomfort, jaw pain, headaches, chewing and digestive issues, ear pain, and even breathing issues. 

A properly aligned bite significantly minimizes these symptoms, promoting better overall oral function. A properly aligned bite leads to a more comfortable day-to-day and contributes to a healthier lifestyle. 

-   **Refined Physical Health**

Investing in your orthodontic health and addressing concerns early prevents more severe orthodontic issues from developing in the future. Orthodontic treatment does so much more than straighten teeth. It acts as a holistic investment in both your oral health and overall well-being. 

Receiving orthodontic treatment like braces or custom clear aligners can correct current issues and prevent future problems from developing. Investing in orthodontic treatment this year with our MyOrthodontist team will improve your aesthetics, function, and comfort, and will have a lasting effect on your confidence.
