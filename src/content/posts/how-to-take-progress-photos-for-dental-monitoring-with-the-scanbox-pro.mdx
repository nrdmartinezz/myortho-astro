#### Your treatment keeps progressing as your doctor monitors your smile remotely, no matter where you are.

#### Be sure you’ve downloaded the [DentalMonitoring (DM) app](https://dental-monitoring.com/for-patients/#GetPatientApp)

#### from the App Store or Google Play Store for free. When your monitoring starts, we’ll send you an activation email with everything you need to log in.

#### Next, you’ll take photos of your smile from anywhere using the ScanBoxpro

####   and send them in through the app.

#### We will receive your photos to review your treatment and ensure it’s on track without the need for unnecessary trips to the office. We will send communication and guidance on your treatment via the app.

#### Over time, you can monitor the progress of your treatment with our photo time lapse technology that shows you how your teeth are moving towards your goal.

####  or call the office near you. Have a great day.
