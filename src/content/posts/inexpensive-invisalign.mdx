Sometimes your dental plan or health insurance will cover all or part of your Invisalign treatment. Sometimes it won’t. And sometimes, it’s not entirely clear.

When you come to MyOrthodontist for your Invisalign treatment, we’ll work with your insurance company to try to get as much covered as possible. We work with most major insurance providers, too (you see can [see a full list here](/insurance/).)

Yes, there are inexpensive Invisalign alternatives—but again, you have to be very careful. Some work the same way Invisalign does, but not all can boast the same results, decade after decade, that Invisalign can.
