### But is Invisalign the Best Choice for You?

**[Invisalign](/types-of-invisalign/)** is a great choice for straightening your teeth without the need for traditional braces.  This can help improve your bite and your smile. No wonder it is quickly becoming the preferred straightener for [**teens**](/blog/10-reasons-to-choose-invisalign-teen/) and [**adults**](/blog/is-invisalign-right-for-me/) who have alignment issues.

**A few quick facts about Invisalign:**

-   The straightener is made out of clear plastic and nearly undetectable, which means you’ll be less conscious about wearing it.
-   Invisalign can still have sharp edges and be hard to work with, even though it involves less pain and discomfort than traditional braces.
-   Patients that have chosen Invisalign have reported having a much easier time cleaning their teeth and have to visit the dentist less often (though they still require some work to maintain.)
