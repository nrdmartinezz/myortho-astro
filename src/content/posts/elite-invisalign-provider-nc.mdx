MyOrthodontist Orthodontics is proud to announce that we were recently named an **Elite Invisalign Provider**. We are honored and humbled to receive such wonderful recognition. We are proud to be the **only Elite Provider** in Cary, Chapel Hill, Durham, Mount Airy and Burgaw; and one of only two Elite Providers in Raleigh and Wake Forest.

### An Elite Provider of Invisalign!

As an Elite Preferred Provider, you must achieve the highest level of Invisalign case experience. It’s not something every orthodontist office achieves, as they rank **in the top 2 percent** of Invisalign providers in the North American region.

-   To be considered an Elite Preferred Invisalign Provider, the orthodontist office must treat among **the highest number of cases** using the [Invisalign clear braces system](http://www.invisalign.com/).
-   Having this distinction helps patients find an Invisalign provider they can trust – one that has the knowledge and experience to create healthier, straighter smiles.
-   Elite Invisalign providers have a **proven track record** of successfully treating patients of all ages.

If you’re looking for an orthodontist for either you or your child, and want a preferred Invisalign provider that you can trust, be sure to choose an orthodontist **that ranks well** among the Invisalign organization.
