Dental Veneers are wafer-thin shells of porcelain that are tooth colored and affixed to the front of the teeth. Once in place, they hide stains, discolorations, and any flaws that may comprise the look of your smile. Before you elect to undergo the procedure, it’s essential that you understand the pros and cons of dental veneers so that you can make the best choice for your smile and oral health.

### What Are the Pros and Cons of Dental Veneers?

#### PROS

**[Appearance](https://www.sharecare.com/health/healthy-teeth-and-mouth/how-healthy-smile-affect-confidence)**: Even if you are diligent about brushing and flossing your smile probably doesn’t look as good as it used to. Dental veneers are an effective way to turn back the clock and hide even serious blemishes.

**Stain Resistance:** Natural teeth pick up stains from the things we eat and drink. The porcelain surface of dental veneers is very stain resistant, meaning it won’t change color if you regularly drink coffee or red wine.

**Durability:** With proper care and maintenance, dental veneers can last up to 20 years.

**Speed:** Compared to other teeth whitening options, dental veneers work almost instantaneously. It typically takes only two visits to install the veneers.

**Utility:** [Dental veneers](/contact/) can correct multiple problems at once – discolored teeth, small gaps, cracked or broken teeth. That means less time spent in the dentist’s chair.

#### CONS

**Eligibility:** Most but not all patients will be eligible to get dental veneers. People with decayed or weakened teeth, advanced periodontal disease, or problems with tooth grinding may be excluded.

**Price:** Depending on the scope of the procedure, the cost of installing dental veneers can be significant, especially when compared to other teeth whitening options.

**Fragility:** Overall, dental veneers are highly durable. But they can break if you grind your teeth, eat hard foods, or don’t care for them properly. Replacing or repairing them can take a lot of time and money.

**Sensitivity:** In order to install the veneers, a thin layer of enamel has to be removed from your teeth. That can cause the teeth to become more sensitive to extreme temperatures.
