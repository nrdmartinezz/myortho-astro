### What’s There to Know About Invisalign?

-   There is less pain and inconvenience compared to traditional braces.
-   The clear plastic straightener is nearly undetectable, which leaves you feeling less self-conscious.
-   Invisalign users have a much easier time cleaning their teeth and have to visit the dentist less often (though they still require some work to maintain.)
-   Invisalign works best when you work with an “Elite” Invisalign Provider.
