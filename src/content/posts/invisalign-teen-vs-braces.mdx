If your child needs an orthodontic correction to ensure they have a bright smile for years to come, there’s good news. You no longer have to resort to **expensive, painful, and unsightly** braces. Invisalign Teen is an effective treatment that offers all of the benefits of braces while presenting none of the drawbacks.

### The Teen Alternative to Braces: Invisalign Teen vs. Braces

Your teen will appreciate this alternative, and you will too when you see how quickly their smile transforms. Read through this guide comparing **Invisalign Teen vs. braces**, and decide if Invisalign Teen is the right course of treatment for your child.

#### How Does Invisalign Teen Work?

Invisalign Teen is based on the same Invisalign orthodontic technology that revolutionized the way patients and doctors treated alignment issues. Made from a firm but flexible material that is also transparent, the Invisalign teen aligners fit around the teeth and subtly **correct their shape over time**. Most alignment issues can be resolved using this technology, which is one of the reasons that it has become so popular so quickly.

#### Why Choose Invisalign Teen?

There are **lots of advantages** to going with Invisalign Teen vs. braces. Both you and your teen will appreciate these Invisalign-exclusive features.

-   Transparent material makes the aligner almost **impossible to see** in the mouth.
-   There are no wires or bands to irritate the gums and palette
-   A wear indicator makes it easy to see when replacement is necessary
-   The **cost is comparable to braces**, and even more affordable in many cases
-   The technology has been proven to be effective
-   The aligner can be removed so that teens can brush and floss normally

#### Why Not Choose Braces?

You know that Invisalign Teen has a lot to offer, but is this system really better than braces? Our patients and their parents tend to think so.

-   Braces are difficult to install and require frequent adjustments.
-   Braces often can’t accommodate for the eruption of canines, second premolars, and second molars.
-   Teens are naturally self-conscious, and braces are **hard to overlook**, causing many teens to be embarrassed and withdrawn at a sensitive time their life.
-   Braces make normal oral care a hassle.
