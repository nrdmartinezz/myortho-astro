**Fix your alignment issues in the way that works best for you.** The average person smiles at least 20 times a day. And when a big grin does break out, it becomes the focal point of the face. That’s good news for people who have straight teeth and a radiant smile. But for people who have tooth alignment issues and feel self-conscious about the way they look, outward expressions of happiness can cause more shame than joy.

### Considering Your Options for How to Get Straight Teeth

If you find yourself covering your mouth or avoiding smiling altogether, **you have two options for how to get straight teeth**. It’s worth considering the pros and cons of both:

#### Pros and Cons of [Traditiona](/types-of-braces/)[l](/types-of-braces/) [Braces](/types-of-braces/)

**Pros**

-   **Cost**: Braces that utilize metal wires and brackets are the least expensive, but only in some cases. In others, the cost of treating alignment issues can be significant.
-   **Utility**: Almost all teeth can be straightened using this treatment option.

**Cons**

-   **Looks**: Despite recent improvements, traditional braces remain quite noticeable and far from attractive. This is often an issue for image-conscious teens or for adults who want to project a professional appearance.
-   **Feel:** Having a mouth full of metal is simply uncomfortable. Patients often complain about the pain of the straightening process and irritation caused to the tongue, teeth, and gums.
-   **Convenience:** In order to protect the brackets and wires patients will need to avoid certain foods and take extra precautions when brushing and flossing.

#### [Learn About Braces](/types-of-braces/)

#### Pros and Cons of Invisalign

**Pros**

-   **Looks**: Invisalign straighteners are made of clear plastic that is nearly invisible to observers. That makes it much easier to pursue a better smile discreetly.
-   **Comfort**: The plastic is also soft and relatively pliable, causing the wearer much less discomfort than traditional braces.
-   **Convenience**: The straighteners can be easily taken out at meal times when brushing and flossing, or while playing sports or instruments. Patients are able to get straighter teeth without changing their normal routine.

**Cons**

-   **Cost**: Invisalign can be expensive, but is not necessarily more expensive than the alternatives. And when the quality-of-life benefits are factored in, the overall value may actually be better.
-   **Utility**: Not all patients will be able to take advantage of Invisalign. Some alignment issues cannot be corrected with the straighteners, and in general young children are ineligible for the treatment.
