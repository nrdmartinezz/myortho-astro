Google “dentist near me” and you’ll likely find a lot of options. But not every local dentist offers the same services, technologies, and treatment planning designed to meet your unique oral health needs, cosmetic preferences, and budget.

### How to Find the Right Dentist Near Me

When looking for a new dentist for you and your family, consider the following before making an appointment:

#### Services

Not all general dentists offer the same services, so it’s important to understand what treatment options are available. Before you decide on a family dentist, learn more about the range of services offered. This is especially important if your treatment plan includes tooth replacement because not all general dentists offer specialized treatments like **[dental implants](/blog/tooth-replacement-options/)** (MyOrthodontist does).

If you’re interested in cosmetic treatments like veneers and teeth whitening, be sure to inquire about those specifically, as well. Not all family dentists offer the same range of treatment options.

#### Range of Care

Coordinating oral care among general dentists, periodontists, oral surgeons, orthodontists and other specialists can be frustrating. Minimize stress by looking for a dental practice that offers a full range of care. MyOrthodontist offers a complete range of care with general, cosmetic, and orthodontic services offered at 11 **[general dentistry and orthodontic offices in North Carolina](/locations/)**.

#### Technology

Dental technology is evolving faster than ever before, and it’s important to make sure your dentist is up to date on the latest innovations. Some newer technologies, like digital x-rays, improve safety and reduce risk, while others like buffering systems can help minimize discomfort. At MyOrthodontist, you’ll find the latest technology combined with the friendly, personalized care of a local neighborhood dental office.

#### Affordability

It’s no secret that dental care can be expensive, but putting off dental care can lead to more costly dental problems in the future. Whether you’re interested in routine exams and cleanings, or require more significant treatment, be sure to ask the dental staff about insurance, payment options, and policies. Some offices (like MyOrthodontist) will even offer a same-day discount on payment by cash, check, or debit card.
