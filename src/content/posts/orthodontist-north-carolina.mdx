### Invisalign

Invisalign is the “invisible” [alternative to traditional braces](/types-of-invisalign/). Invisalign uses a series of clear, custom-made aligners to gradually move your teeth into their desired position. These aligners are virtually invisible, comfortable to wear, and easy for you to remove for special occasions.

Invisalign is a great option for teens who need braces (but don’t want to be seen wearing them) and for adults (who might have put off braces due to the stigma and inconvenience). It’s also the perfect option for anyone who worries that brackets and wires will prove to be too restrictive or too painful.

### Lingual Braces

Lingual braces use the same metal brackets and wires as traditional braces, but are installed on the inside of your teeth to keep them hidden. This gives you the benefits of traditional braces, but makes them less apparent while speaking or smiling.

[Read here to see whether lingual braces are right for you](/blog/braces-behind-teeth/).

### Ceramic Braces

Ceramic braces are similar to metal braces in shape and size, but use tooth-colored or clear ceramic brackets that blend more naturally into your teeth. Some even use tooth-colored wires to keep your treatment even lower profile. You can read more about the differences and advantages in our post “[Ceramic Braces Vs. Traditional Braces: What’s The Difference?](/blog/ceramic-braces-vs-traditional/)”
