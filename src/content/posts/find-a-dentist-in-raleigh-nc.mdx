**Your choice has a big impact on your care.** If you’re looking for a dentist in Raleigh, NC, you have dozens of options. That means the difficult part is not finding a dentist, but finding the *right* dentist. While all of them will be qualified, not all of them will be capable of providing you and your family with the **service, care, and long-term relationship that you’re looking for**. Before you make an appointment anywhere, make sure your dentist in Raleigh, NC has these qualities:

### Find the Right Dentist in Raleigh, NC

#### A Complete Range of Care

One of the most frustrating things about oral care is that you have to find a dentist, and then possibly an orthodontist, pediatric dentist, periodontist, and other specialists. That can be a time and labor-intensive process. The best policy is to form a relationship with a dentist in Raleigh, NC that can **handle the widest range of your entire family’s oral care needs**.

#### A Focus on Painless Dentistry

No dentist sets out to inflict pain, but some are definitely more gentle than others. Rather than trying to guess which kind you are making an appointment with, seek out one that specializes in painless dentistry. Using a variety of careful techniques along with state-of-the-art equipment, this style of dentistry is able to **minimize stress and pain for the patient** while still ensuring their smile is healthy and bright.

#### A Total Commitment to Service

Service from your dentist in Raleigh, NC starts the minute you first contact the office. You should be able to **get your questions answered clearly, book appointments around your schedule**, and make special accommodations if necessary. It continues as you sit in the waiting room, work with the hygienists and dentists, and figure out your payment options. At every step in the process, you should be treated with respect, and if at any time you’re not, you’re seeing the wrong dentist.

#### A Drive to Keep Dentistry Affordable

The cost of oral care can be significant, especially as kids start getting braces and older adults deal with advanced issues. Don’t work with a dentist in Raleigh, NC whose goal is to inflate your bill as much as possible. Instead, look for one that makes it **easy to work with your [insurance](/insurance/)**[,](/insurance/) that offers flexible payment options, and that provides discounts for same-day payment.
