### Fun Facts You May Not Know About Invisalign:

-   The straightener is made out of clear plastic and nearly undetectable, which eliminates the embarrassment of wearing it.
-   The pain and inconvenience usually involved are significantly less compared to braces.
-   Invisalign users have a much easier time clearing their teeth and have to visit the dentist less often (though they still require some work to maintain.)
