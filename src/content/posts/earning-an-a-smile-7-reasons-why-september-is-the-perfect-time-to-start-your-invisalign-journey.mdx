As the summer days slowly fade away and the crisp autumn air begins to creep in, September marks a time of new beginnings. For a lot of our patients, it’s the start of a new school year, the reorganization of schedules after vacation. For residents of North Carolina, it’s also the perfect moment to focus on something equally important: your smile.

At MyOrthodontist, we believe that everyone deserves a smile they can confidently share with the world. Whether you’re a student heading back to school, a professional returning to the office, or simply  looking to enhance your appearance, Invisalign can help you achieve a beautiful smile without the hassle and discomfort of traditional braces. Led by the expertise of Dr. Moray, our team has shown countless patients in the North Carolina community the transformative power of Invisalign. Now is the perfect time to start your journey and see the transformation Invisalign and Dr. Moray can give you!

Our team at MyOrthodontist has compiled 7 reasons why September is the best time to kickstart your path to a straighter, healthier smile!

### **1\. Back-to-School Confidence for Students**

Whether you’re heading into middle school, high schooler, or college, September means one thing: school is back in session. It’s the time of year when everyone wants to put their best foot (and smile,) forward. Each new academic year brings new opportunities, friends, and experiences. We want each student to feel fully confident in themselves, but oftentimes a less-than-perfect smile can hinder that confidence and self-esteem.

Invisalign is perfect for students building their self-esteem because it’s a discreet way to straighten teeth without the awkward appearance and uncomfortable irritation of metal braces. Because the clear aligners are nearly invisible, students can continue participating in school activities, sports, and social events without worrying about their appearance. Another great benefit of these aligners is they are removable, allowing students to eat their favorite foods and to easily maintain their oral hygiene routine.

Traditional braces are often a source of discomfort, and the thought of wearing them for years can be really overwhelming for teenagers. Invisalign offers a perfect solution that doesn’t interfere with a student’s lifestyle. Whether it’s the school dance, sports practice, or a club activity, Invisalign provides the flexibility and confidence students need to fully, and happily, participate in their school experience.

Starting Invisalign in September means that by the time the school year is in full swing, you’ll be well on your way to achieving a beautiful, straight smile—giving you a boost of confidence both inside and outside the classroom. And let’s face it, a confident student is often a happier, more successful student!

### **2\. Perfect Timing for Busy Professionals**

For adults and working professionals in North Carolina, September is often a month of renewed focus after the restoration summer brings. It’s a time to get back into the swing of work and to tackle your personal goals, and improving your smile can easily be at the top of that list.

Traditional braces can be a challenge for adults who frequently attend meetings, conferences, or client-facing events. Chunky, metal braces are often a deterrent for working professionals looking to improve their smile, making them feel unpolished or immature. However, Invisalign provides a perfect solution for working professionals. The Invisalign trays are clear and virtually invisible, allowing professionals to maintain their polished appearance during the course of their  orthodontic treatment.

Many professionals are hesitant about orthodontic treatments because of concerns about their appearance during meetings or presentations. With Invisalign, those concerns are alleviated. You can conduct business as usual while discreetly transforming your smile. Whether you’re leading a boardroom discussion or engaging in virtual meetings, your Invisalign treatment will be hidden from your associates.

Starting your treatment in September also aligns perfectly with the end-of-year professional cycles. Many companies conduct reviews or end-of-year evaluations during the last quarter, and having a more aligned smile by then could enhance both your confidence and your overall professional image.

With September marking the beginning of a new quarter, this is the ideal time to commit to a treatment that can align your teeth without disrupting your work or lifestyle. Imagine walking into your next meeting or presentation with the confidence that comes from knowing your smile is improving every day!

### **3\. Flexibility in Scheduling & Routine**

One of the largest advantages of Invisalign is the extreme flexibility it offers patients. Unlike traditional braces, which require more frequent appointments with Dr. Moray for adjustments, Invisalign only requires check-ups approximately every 6-8 weeks. These visits are quick and efficient, allowing you to stay on top of your treatment without it interfering with your daily schedule.

September is a time when most families in North Carolina establish or return to their regular routines, making it easier to incorporate Invisalign appointments into your schedule. Whether you’re juggling school pick-ups, work meetings, or extracurricular activities, you’ll appreciate the convenience of Invisalign’s minimal appointment requirements. The ease of maintenance and shorter office visits fit seamlessly into the hectic back-to-school season most of our families experience.

In fact, many patients at MyOrthodontist appreciate that Invisalign allows them to continue their usual activities without interruption. You don’t have to worry about the inconvenience or discomfort of emergency orthodontic visits for broken brackets or wires. With Invisalign, you’re in control! With virtual guidance from Dr. Moray, you’ll change your aligners every two weeks, allowing your treatment progression to be a smooth and hassle-free process.

For parents with children in treatment, September’s back-to-school timing makes it easier to ensure everyone in the family gets the care they need. Whether it’s coordinating the whole family’s appointments or just making room in your own schedule, the beginning of the school year is a time to recalibrate routines, and Invisalign’s flexibility makes it easier to fit into a busy lifestyle.

### **4\. Prepare for the Holidays with a Confident Smile**

September is the start of the fall  season, and with fall comes the anticipation and preparation of the holidays. From family gatherings to office parties and social events, there are countless opportunities to showcase your improving smile. Starting your Invisalign treatment in September means that by the time the holiday season rolls around, you’ll already see improvements in your teeth alignment.

Imagine smiling confidently for holiday photos, meeting relatives you haven’t seen in a while, or making a great impression at the office holiday party. Invisalign’s discreet design ensures that you’ll be able to enjoy all of these moments without the noticeable presence of metal braces. Another added plus of Invisalign’s removable aligners is that you won’t need to worry about missing out on any holiday treats!

The holiday season is filled with indulgent foods, from sticky caramel apples to candy canes and so many desserts in between. Traditional braces often come with a long list of food restrictions, but with Invisalign, you can simply remove your aligners to enjoy your favorite treats without the worry of damaging your orthodontic equipment. Just remember to brush and floss before inserting your slingers again! This freedom allows you to partake in the holiday festivities without feeling left out, and without distributing your path to a new smile!

By starting your Invisalign journey now, you’ll be setting yourself up to enter the holiday season with improved confidence, knowing that your smile is on its way to perfection. Not only will you look your best, but you’ll also feel great about taking proactive steps towards a healthier, more beautiful smile.

### **5\. Take Advantage of Insurance and Flex Spending Accounts**

Many of our patients’ insurance plans and flexible spending accounts (FSAs) follow the calendar year, with benefits expiring at the end of December. September is a great time to start your Invisalign journey and make use of these benefits before they expire. Most insurance plans provide coverage for orthodontic treatment, and Invisalign is often included. Dr. Moray and the MyOrthodontist team can work with you to navigate your insurance options and help you maximize your benefits.

With many patients able to use their FSA or Health Savings Account (HSA) to pay for their treatment, Invisalign becomes a budget-friendly option. It’s a good idea to review your insurance policy and FSA contributions early in the fall so that you can plan accordingly. Many of our patients actually discover that they have unspent FSA funds they must use before the end of the year. Why not put that money towards a treatment that will benefit your smile for a lifetime?

By starting your treatment in September, you’ll be able to spread the cost of Invisalign over the remaining months of the year, making it easier to fit into your budget. Plus, many patients use their remaining FSA to cover the cost of treatment, ensuring they don’t lose out on any funds set aside for healthcare expenses. Our team at MyOrthodontist is here to help with any financial questions you have about Invisalign. Feel free to call our office to further discuss any of these options and to find a plan that financially benefits your family.

### **6\. Custom, Comfortable Treatment with Advanced Technology**

Invisalign isn’t just about aesthetics, it’s also about comfort. Unlike traditional braces with bulky, poky brackets and wires that can cause irritation, Invisalign aligners are made from smooth, medical-grade plastic that is intricately designed to fit comfortably over your teeth. The advanced technology used in Invisalign treatments ensures that your aligners are crafted to the exact specifications of your unique smile and orthodontic needs.

September is an ideal time to visit Dr. Moray in North Carolina for a consultation to see how Invisalign can work for you. Using cutting-edge 3D scanning technology, Dr. Moray will create a digital model of your teeth, allowing you to see a preview of your future smile before even starting treatment. This personalized approach guarantees a comfortable and efficient treatment experience from day one.

### **7\. A Boost in Oral Health**

At MyOrthodontist, it’s important for our patients to know that straightening your teeth isn’t just about improving your appearance—it’s also about enhancing your overall oral health. Crooked or crowded teeth can make it difficult to maintain proper oral hygiene, leading to an increased risk of cavities, gum disease, and other dental issues. Invisalign helps align your teeth, making it easier to brush, floss, and keep your mouth healthy.
