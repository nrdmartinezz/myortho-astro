## About

Dr. Daniel Levy is a board-certified Orthodontist who worked in academia for almost 20 years and trained over 40 orthodontic residents in the United States. Dr. Levy has been a member of the MyOrthodontist family since he arrived in North Carolina. He earned his dental degree and Master of Science in Pediatric Dentistry in Venezuela. Dr Levy also obtained a Master's in Dental Sciences and a Certificate in Orthodontics from the University of Puerto Rico, followed by specialized training in managing cleft lip and palate patients.

He is actively involved in publications and research related to orthodontics, pediatric dentistry, cleft lip and palate diagnosis, and management. He has also lectured at national and international meetings, focusing on these same dental and medical conditions.

Amara is his wife and colleague. They have two wonderful boys, Marcus and Danny. In their spare time, they love to travel, spend time with family and friends, and enjoy soccer games.
