## About

Dr. Bethany Doolittle grew up in the suburbs of Minneapolis and St. Paul, Minnesota. She attended the University of Iowa for her undergraduate studies, where she also played collegiate basketball.

Following graduation, she spent two years playing professional basketball overseas in France and Switzerland before returning to pursue her further education.

Dr. Doolittle went on to earn her Master of Biomedical Sciences degree from Midwestern University in Illinois and later returned to the University of Iowa to complete her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree. She then continued her training at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, where she completed her orthodontic residency.

Outside the office, Dr. Doolittle enjoys traveling, trying new foods (whether cooking at home or exploring new restaurants), watching and playing sports, and spending time outdoors.
