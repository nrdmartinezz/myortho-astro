## About

Dr. Chris Devantier was born in Denmark and raised in southern Utah. He earned his undergraduate degree from Brigham Young University in Provo, then went on to graduate cum laude from the University of Nevada, Las Vegas School of Dental Medicine. He completed his orthodontic residency in Jacksonville, Florida, where he received advanced training in the latest orthodontic techniques and technologies.

Dr. Devantier is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO) and is committed to staying current with the most effective and innovative treatments in orthodontic care.

Outside of work, he enjoys spending time with his wife and their three young children. He loves being outside and especially enjoys hiking, camping, and fishing.
