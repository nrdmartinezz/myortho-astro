## About

Dr. Nathaly Lemoine was born in the Dominican Republic and moved to Charlotte, North Carolina, where she spent her formative years. Her academic journey began at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, where she earned a Bachelor of Science in Chemistry.

Dr. Lemoine continued her education at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, where she received her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree.

Seeking to specialize further, she pursued an orthodontic residency at Texas A&M University's Baylor College of Dentistry in Dallas, Texas.

In addition to her professional pursuits, Dr. Lemoine maintains a well-rounded lifestyle. She values time spent with her Doberman, Xena, and enjoys engaging in board games with friends. On Sundays, she follows Formula 1 racing, and she also finds relaxation and inspiration in hiking the picturesque mountains of North Carolina.
