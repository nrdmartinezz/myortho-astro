## About

Originally from Michigan, Dr. Kang attended the prestigious University of Michigan (Go Blue!) to study Neuroscience. She then attended and graduated at the top of her class from the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine. During her studies, she was inducted into the prestigious Omicron Kappa Omicron Upsilon (OKU) National Dental Honor Society for her academic achievements. She then completed her orthodontic training at St. Louis University, where she had in-depth training in traditional and contemporary orthodontic treatments and appliances.

When Dr. Kang is not creating beautiful smiles for her patients, she enjoys spending time with her family, exploring new cuisines and restaurants, and live comedy shows. Her new favorite cuisine is Ethiopian, and you should try it too!

We believe Dr. Kang’s knowledge and passion for orthodontics make her an amazing member of our team and will help us provide the best personalized orthodontic care for you.
