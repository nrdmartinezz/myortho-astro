## About

Dr. Nate Goldsberry is from Salt Lake City, Utah. After a two year service mission in Mexico, he earned his undergraduate degree from Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah.

He then attended Loma Linda University in Southern California for Dental school and Orthodontics residency where he received advanced training in the latest orthodontic techniques and technologies.

Outside of work, Dr. Goldsberry enjoys spending time with his wife and two sons. He is an avid football and basketball fan, and he loves spending time outdoors and playing sports with his kids.

Dr. Goldsberry is passionate about serving in his community, and loves helping his patients achieve healthy, beautiful smiles.
