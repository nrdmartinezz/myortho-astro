## About

Dr. Kubala, a South Carolina native, has been practicing orthodontics for 19 years, following six years in general dentistry. His dental journey began in the U.S. Army, where he was first stationed at Fort Campbell, KY, before serving in various locations worldwide.

Dr. Kubala later pursued specialized orthodontic training in sunny Jacksonville, FL. His training, combined with years of hands-on experience, allows him to create personalized treatment plans that improve both function and aesthetics. With a strong background in general dentistry and orthodontics, Dr. Kubala is well-equipped to help children, teenagers, and adults achieve healthy, confident smiles. He takes the time to educate his patients, ensuring they feel comfortable and informed throughout their treatment.

Outside of work, Dr. Kubala is a devoted husband and proud father of four. He values the opportunity to serve the community of Eastern North Carolina and is passionate about helping patients of all ages achieve beautiful, lasting smiles.
